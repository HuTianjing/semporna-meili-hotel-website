## Category 1: Reservation & Booking · 预订与付款

**Q: How may I secure my reservation at Meili Resort Hotel?**

A: Your journey to our private sea can be arranged seamlessly. We warmly recommend booking via our official website to secure the most advantageous terms. Alternatively, reservations may be made through esteemed Online Travel Agents, such as Trip.com, Booking.com, or by contacting our reservations team directly.

- WhatsApp: +6011 2780 3997
- Email: amy@meilihotel.com

---

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**

A: To ensure a smooth and secure transaction, we accept payments via online bank transfer or major credit and debit cards. Specific details will be provided upon confirmation of your booking.

---

**Q: Is a deposit required?**

A: Yes, a deposit is required to formally secure your booking and sanctuary with us. This confirms our mutual commitment to your forthcoming retreat. All payment particulars will be clearly communicated following your reservation confirmation.

---

## Category 2: Arrival & Stay Details · 抵达与入住

**Q: What are your check-in and check-out times?**

A: To allow for the meticulous preparation of your villa, check-in is welcomed from **2:00 PM** onwards. We kindly request that check-out be completed by **12:00 PM**, ensuring every guest enjoys the same pristine welcome.

> Requests for early check-in or late departure are graciously considered based on availability and may incur an additional charge.

---

**Q: How do I reach the resort?**

A: Your transition from the everyday to the extraordinary is part of the experience. Complimentary land and boat transfers are included in your package, guiding you gracefully from Semporna to our private island realm.

---

**Q: Is airport or jetty transfer provided?**

A: Indeed, we provide a seamless transfer service. To ensure our team awaits you without delay, please share your complete arrival details with us in advance.

---

**Q: What should I bring?**

A: We advise bringing:

- Passport or identification
- Your booking confirmation
- Personal medications
- Swimwear and sunscreen (essential for our aquatic paradise)
- Comfortable, non-slip shoes (recommended for boat transfers)

---

## Category 3: Policies & Amenities · 政策与设施

**Q: What is your cancellation policy?**

A: We understand plans may change. Our policy is designed with fairness in mind: free cancellation is permitted up to a specified number of days prior to arrival (please refer to your booking terms). Late cancellations or no-shows will incur a charge, and some exclusive offers may be non-refundable.

---

**Q: Are breakfast and meals included?**

A: Yes, your stay includes a full board culinary journey. Breakfast, lunch, and dinner are thoughtfully prepared by our chefs and are part of your package, allowing you to dine with complete ease. Please verify your specific booking details.

---

**Q: Is complimentary Wi-Fi available?**

A: Yes, complimentary Wi-Fi is provided throughout our public spaces and within your villa, allowing you to share moments of your serene escape should you wish.

---

**Q: Is smoking permitted?**

A: In commitment to preserving our pure, natural environment and the comfort of all our guests, smoking is respectfully permitted in **designated outdoor areas only**.

---

## Category 4: Facilities & Services · 设施与服务

**Q: What exclusive facilities do you offer?**

A: Your private sea sanctuary is complemented by a host of curated amenities:

- Infinity swimming pool
- Overwater restaurant and bars
- Five-Star Diving Club (五星潜水俱乐部) — guided exploration
- Above-water golf driving range
- Comprehensive gym
- Wellness centre and spa
- Bespoke tour arrangements to the surrounding wonders

---

**Q: Do you provide special arrangements for honeymoons or celebrations?**

A: Absolutely. We delight in honouring life's special moments. Please inform us of your celebration in advance, and our team will be honoured to propose bespoke arrangements to make your stay even more memorable. Supplementary charges may apply.

---

## Category 5: Family & Practicalities · 家庭出行

**Q: Are children welcome?**

A: Families are most welcome. Children aged **5 and below** may stay free of charge when sharing existing bedding. The vibrant spirit of discovery here is a joy for all ages.

---

**Q: Do you provide extra beds?**

A: Yes, an extra bed can be arranged in your villa upon request, subject to availability and an additional charge.

---

**Q: Is parking available?**

A: As your journey to us concludes with a serene boat transfer to our island, parking is not required nor provided at the jetty departure point.

---

**Q: Are pets allowed?**

A: With profound respect for the delicate local ecosystem and for the comfort of all our guests, we regret that **pets are not permitted** on the island.

---

## Category 6: Modifications & Contact · 修改预订与联系我们

**Q: Can I modify my reservation dates?**

A: We will always endeavour to accommodate your plans. Date modifications are subject to villa availability and must be requested **at least 3 days** prior to your original arrival date.

**Q: How do I modify my existing booking?**
