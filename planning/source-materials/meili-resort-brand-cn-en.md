## 二、绝世位置 · Unrivaled Location

MEILI 度假酒店坐落于北纬 4° 具有"珊瑚金三角"之称的马来西亚仙本那玻璃海之上，与著名的世界潜水点诗巴丹为邻。这里拥有全球最多样化的海洋生物，被誉为海洋的摇篮与基因库，这片如同液态绿松石般的玻璃海以其震撼的原始之美，吸引着全世界的目光。

Meili Resort Hotel is situated on the glass-like sea of Semporna, Malaysia — at 4° North latitude, within the famed "Coral Triangle." It neighbours Sipadan Island, one of the world's premier diving destinations. Home to the most diverse marine life on Earth, this turquoise sea is celebrated as the cradle and gene pool of the ocean, attracting global attention with its breathtaking, pristine beauty.

## 三、天然环境与人文 · Nature & Culture

仙本那以其水晶般清澈如玻璃的海水而享誉世界，海水呈现出层层叠叠的青绿色，能见度极高。

Semporna is celebrated for its crystalline, glass-like waters, which display layers of turquoise and boast exceptional visibility.

### 珊瑚礁生态系统 · Coral Reef Ecosystem

- 栖息着高达 3,000 种珊瑚礁鱼类，占全球总量的 37%
- 拥有高达 600 种珊瑚种类，占全球总量的 76%

Remarkably, this single location hosts up to 3,000 species of coral reef fish, which accounts for 37% of all such species worldwide. Up to 600 species of coral can be found here, constituting 76% of the global total — a concentration found nowhere else on Earth.

### 有"风下之乡"之称 · Land Below the Wind

仙本那海位于北纬 4°~5°、东经 118°~119°，靠近赤道，常年气温 26°~32°，属热带地区。因被菲律宾棉兰老岛和加里曼丹岛陆地遮挡，是台风路径的盲区，过去百年无台风灾害，成为地理学上的幸运儿，"风下之乡"由此而来。

The Semporna Sea is situated at approximately 4° to 5° North latitude and 118° to 119° East longitude, near the equator. With a consistent year-round temperature of 26° to 32°C, it lies within the tropics. Sheltered by the landmasses of Mindanao in the Philippines and Kalimantan, the area lies in a blind spot for typhoon tracks. Having recorded no typhoon disasters for over a century, it is considered a geographical rarity — hence the name "Land Below the Wind."

### 标志性岛屿与水下地貌 · Iconic Islands & Underwater Landscapes

世界级潜水圣地诗巴丹岛，陡峭的海底悬崖和丰富的海底生物吸引无数潜水爱好者。

The world-renowned diving paradise of Sipadan Island attracts countless diving enthusiasts with its sheer underwater cliffs and abundant marine life.

### 人文特色 · Cultural Heritage

如此的梦幻秘境同时也养育着淳朴的海上巴瑶族人，擅长自由潜水，徒手下潜数十米，被称为海上游牧民族，独特的巴瑶族文化为仙本那这片海增添了一抹人间色彩。极致自然与鲜活人文的交织带来无比的视觉震撼——从海底珊瑚公园到海面椰林落日，再到巴瑶族孩子跃入碧海的瞬间，处处是生命力的展现。

This ethereal paradise is also home to the simple, seafaring Bajau people. Masters of freediving, they can descend dozens of metres unaided and are known as the "sea nomads." Their unique culture adds a profound human dimension to the waters of Semporna. The interplay of pristine nature and vibrant living culture creates a layered narrative of immense visual power — a vivid tableau of life, from the coral gardens below to the sunset over coconut palms, and the moment a Bajau child leaps into the cerulean sea.

MEILI 度假酒店的诞生源于对大海的敬畏与挚爱，承载着亿万人的旅行梦想与探索渴望。当地中海的微风拂过橄榄树林，当威尼斯的水波第一次映出朝霞，她诞生了——古老的旅游女神"MEILI"将迅捷赠与她，伟大旅行家徐霞客的精神是她的灵魂，保护并探索着这片海洋生命的"摇篮"和"基因库"。

The birth of Meili Resort Hotel stems from a reverence and deep affection for the sea, carrying the travel dreams and thirst for exploration of countless people. When the Mediterranean breeze brushed through olive groves, and the waters of Venice first mirrored the dawn glow, she came into being. The ancient goddess of travel, "MEILI," bestowed her with swiftness, while the spirit of the great explorer Xu Xiake became her soul — protecting and exploring this "cradle" and "gene pool" of marine life.

## 六、安全、环保与奢华 · Safety, Sustainability & Luxury

### 建筑选材的奢华 · Luxury Building Materials

酒店建筑用材主要以天然木材为主，选用上万吨天然纯木 **BELIAN** 打造。BELIAN 常用于部落首领的权杖、勇士的刀柄，以及被雕刻的护身符，被称为"铁木"，以其耐久性和抗生物侵害能力著称。它含有特殊的树脂和油脂，具有抗真菌、抗腐蚀的作用，在东南亚原住民文化中，BELIAN 是一种具有强大精神力量和神圣地位的物质，也是华人文化中的辟邪木，象征着稳固与接地、保护与净化、持久与毅力。其极强耐腐蚀性有长达百年的使用时限，越泡水越硬的特性，大程度体现了水上建筑物的安全和持久耐用。

The hotel is primarily constructed from natural timber — tens of thousands of tonnes of pure **BELIAN** wood. Known as "ironwood," BELIAN is celebrated for its durability and resistance to biological attack. It contains special resins and oils that provide anti-fungal and anti-corrosive properties. In Southeast Asian indigenous culture, BELIAN holds great spiritual power and sacred status. In Chinese culture, it is regarded as a protective wood, symbolising stability, protection, and endurance. Its extreme corrosion resistance gives it a lifespan of over a century, and it grows harder when submerged — making it ideal for overwater structures.

### 独立的环保发电系统 · Independent Eco-Friendly Power Generation

酒店客房 100% 采用太阳能系统，用于照明、热水、空调等，确保不使用化学燃料，不会因排放污染海洋环境，坚守酒店的全面环保理念。

All hotel guest rooms are 100% powered by our solar energy system, providing lighting, hot water, air conditioning, and more. This ensures no chemical fuels are used and prevents any emissions from polluting the marine environment, upholding the hotel's comprehensive environmental philosophy.

### 最高标准的海水净化系统 · Highest Standard Seawater Purification System

酒店采用反渗透技术——全球最顶级的海水净化系统。通过抽取海水，利用高科技全自动分子过滤技术，经过层层过滤，去除杂质、盐分、微生物，让海水变成弱碱性健康饮用水。过滤出来的浓缩盐水通过专业打散技术重回大海；使用过的废水根据不同用途，回到污水处理中心，通过微生物技术净化后排回大海，确保不给大海带来额外负担。

We operate a fully independent water system. Seawater is purified into drinking water through reverse osmosis and molecular filtration. All concentrated brine and wastewater are treated on-site via specialised diffusion and microbial processes, ensuring every drop is returned to the ocean cleanly, with no environmental burden.

### 独立的消防系统 · Independent Fire Protection System

大截面木材在遇火时表面会形成炭化层，有效隔绝氧气并降低热量传导，保护内部结构，其耐火极限甚至优于在高温下容易软化崩塌的钢结构。选用的结构木材均经过真空加压阻燃处理，阻燃剂深入木材纤维内部，达到国家 B1 级难燃标准，从根本上降低火焰蔓延速度，为疏散争取黄金时间。此外，配备紧急疏散广播、高压喷淋以及物理防火封堵措施，确保全面安全。

Our timber structures utilise large-section wood's natural property to form an insulating char layer during fire, providing fire resistance that can exceed steel. All timber is vacuum-pressure treated with fire retardants to meet Class B1 inflammability standards, significantly slowing combustion and enabling safe evacuation. We supplement this with full fire systems: emergency broadcast, high-pressure sprinklers, and physical fire-blocking seals.

### 海警长期安全管理体系 · Government-Backed Security Management

仙本那是马来西亚的旅游名片，美丽度假酒店作为仙本那第一个五星标准度假酒店，马来西亚政府特指派海警长期驻扎酒店，在全世界都属罕见，为酒店安全提供了最高保障。

As the premier tourist destination of Malaysia, Semporna is home to the Meili Resort Hotel, its first five-star standard resort. In a demonstration of exceptional commitment to security, the Malaysian government has designated a unit of the Maritime Police for a permanent station at the hotel — a measure exceptionally rare worldwide. This provides an unparalleled layer of safety and assurance for our guests.

### 紧急避险场地设施 · Emergency Shelter and Supporting Facilities

仙本那被称为风下之乡，无台风地震困扰。即便如此，酒店建筑仍按照可抵抗 7 级地震、10 级台风的标准设计，紧急庇护处的设计更达到双倍标准，更安心，更放心。

Known as the "Land Below the Wind," Semporna is blessedly free from typhoons and earthquakes. Despite this, our structures are reinforced to withstand magnitude-7 earthquakes and category-10 typhoons. Our purpose-built refuge is designed to double these standards, offering our guests profound peace of mind.

### 独立的垃圾污水处理系统 · Independent Waste and Sewage Treatment System

我们建立了完全独立的垃圾与污水闭环处理系统。所有废弃物均在岛内通过微生物分解、物理分离等科技进行无害化处理，净化后的水达到安全标准后方才回归自然，杜绝对海洋生态的干扰。

We operate a fully independent, closed-loop waste and sewage treatment system. All waste is processed on-site through technologies such as microbial digestion and physical separation. The purified water is only returned to the environment after meeting stringent safety standards, ensuring absolutely no impact on the marine ecosystem.
